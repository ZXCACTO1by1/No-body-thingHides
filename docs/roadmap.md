# Roadmap

Roadmap items are proposals, not shipped features or delivery promises.

## Under consideration

- Face and avatar inputs with explicit consent and strict privacy boundaries.
- Authorized phone-identifier workflows limited to lawful, provider-supported data.
- More public provider contracts and clearer source-health diagnostics.
- Reproducible Windows fresh-OS acceptance and contributor-friendly fixtures.
- Better documentation for replacing or extending the public client.

## Explicitly out of scope for 1.0.0

- Universal person identification.
- Private account access, login or CAPTCHA bypasses.
- Population-wide recall claims.
- Native Telegram MTProto transport.
- A public rebuild of the private core.
